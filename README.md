# zepto-gtm-strategy
A product and go-to-market case study on how Zepto can grow in India's quick commerce market, built from my own user research.

## Problem
Quick commerce is growing fast, but users show almost no loyalty to any app. Zepto leads on speed but has about 29% share against Blinkit's roughly 50%, and little presence outside the metros.

## What I did
- Ran a 50-respondent primary survey (switching triggers, order behaviour, Zepto Pass intent)
- Built 3 personas with jobs-to-be-done and pain points
- Sized the market (TAM/SAM/SOM) and analysed competitors (Porter's 5 Forces, positioning map, capability scorecard)
- Scored 6 acquisition channels with a weighted model
- Modelled 3 GMV scenarios and a KPI dashboard in Google Sheets
- Wrote a 90-day launch plan with KPIs for each phase

## Key findings
- 60% of respondents order 3+ times a week and use 2.27 apps on average
- Hidden charges were the top complaint; discounts and prices were as strong a switching trigger as speed
- 82% are open to a subscription pass (28% definite, 54% maybe)

## Recommendation
Launch in 10 Tier 2 cities within 90 days, lead with a "no hidden charges" promise, and build loyalty through referrals and Zepto Pass. The recommended scenario models about +129% annual GMV vs the status quo (a modelled projection, not a measured result).

## Limitations
Small sample (n=50) skewed towards young Delhi users, only 4 Tier 2 respondents, and assumptions in the model that need real-world testing. The case study lists next steps (a pilot, A/B tests and a larger Tier 2 survey).
